# 2. Administrator duties

| How often | Task | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Every sign-in | Confirm you are on the correct account (name and role in the left panel). | Sidebar “Signed in as” |
| Weekly | Skim **Dashboard** counts and **Analytics** for unusual drops. | chapters 4, 16 |
| Weekly | Review **Posts** comments and **Journal** entries that may need deletion. | chapters 5, 12 |
| When someone joins | Add the person on **Users** and give the smallest role that fits the job. | chapter 17 |
| When someone leaves | Delete or demote the account the same day. | chapter 17 |
| When a hotline changes | Update **Care Support** and test the Philippine phone format. | chapter 11 |
| When app wording is wrong | Edit **App Translations** (UI labels) — not the post Body. | chapter 20 |
| Monthly | Walk **Settings** legal pages (English and Tagalog Body). | chapter 18 |

## 2.1 Which role to give

| Role | What they can open |
|---|---|
| Super Admin | Everything, including Users, Analytics, and Journal. |
| Publisher | Content, rehab centers, contests, IEC, care toolkit/checks/support, Hope, notifications, settings, lookups, translations, word blocking. **Not** Users, Analytics, or Journal. |
| Editor | **Posts** only. |
| Analytics Viewer | **Analytics** only. |

> **Tip.** Most content staff should be **Publisher**, not Super Admin. Keep Super Admin for a small IT / records set.

## 2.2 When someone joins

1. Click **Users**, then **Add User**.
2. Fill name, work email, role, and a temporary password that meets the policy.
3. Tell them they must change the password on first sign-in.
4. Ask them to confirm they can open only the menus they need.

## 2.3 When someone leaves

1. Click **Users**.
2. Find the row, click the trash **Delete User** action (or right-click the row → Delete).
3. Confirm in the dialog. A toast confirms the account is gone.
